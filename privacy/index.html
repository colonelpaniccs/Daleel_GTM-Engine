Privacy

What this site collects, what happens to anything you send us, and how to reach us about it.

Last updated 23 September 2026

Who we are

This site, daleel.fyi, is run by daleel. For any question about your information, or to use any of the rights described below, email privacy@daleel.fyi.

What the site collects on its own

The site sets no cookies and runs no analytics, tracking pixels or advertising tools, and its fonts are served from the site itself rather than from a third party. It is hosted on GitHub Pages, and GitHub logs the IP address of every visitor for security purposes. We don’t control or receive that log, and GitHub’s handling of it is covered by the GitHub General Privacy Statement.

What the contact form collects

When you send the contact form, we receive what you enter: your name, work email, company, company website if you add one, location, regional presence, sector, sales motion, CRM and any notes. Everything except the website and notes is required, because we use those answers to understand your inquiry and reply to it.

How we use it

We use it for one thing, which is replying to your inquiry and talking with you about whether daleel fits your team. We don’t sell it, share it for advertising or use it to make automated decisions about you.

Where it goes

Your submission is delivered through Microsoft Azure, running in a United States region, to our Microsoft 365 mailbox, which is also stored in the United States. Microsoft processes it for us under the Microsoft Products and Services Data Protection Addendum, and no one else receives it. This means your information leaves your country and is kept in the United States, where data protection law may differ from the law where you live.

Why we can use it

We use your information because you asked us to get back to you, and with your consent, which you give by ticking the box on the form before you send it. You can withdraw your consent at any time by emailing privacy@daleel.fyi, which doesn’t affect anything we did with your information before you withdrew it.

How long we keep it

We keep form submissions for up to 24 months after our last contact with you and then delete them, or sooner if you ask us to.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you can ask to see the information we hold about you, correct it, delete it, object to how we use it or withdraw your consent. Email privacy@daleel.fyi and we’ll respond within the time your local law requires. You can also complain to your data protection regulator, which in Saudi Arabia is the Saudi Data & AI Authority (SDAIA).

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we’ll post the new version here and update the date at the top.
