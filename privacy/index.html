Privacy

Last updated 23 September 2026

Who We Are

This site, daleel.fyi, is operated by the daleel team (“daleel,” “we,” “us,” or “our”). Questions regarding this policy, or requests to exercise the rights described below, may be directed to privacy@daleel.fyi.

Information We Collect Automatically

The site does not set cookies and does not run analytics, tracking pixels, or advertising tools. Fonts are served directly from the site rather than from a third party. Our hosting provider logs the IP address of each visitor as part of operating the site; this log is not controlled by, or provided to, us.

Information Collected Through the Contact Form

When you submit the contact form, we receive the information you provide, including your contact and company details. This information is used to understand and respond to your inquiry.

How We Use and Share This Information

This information is used solely to respond to your inquiry and to assess whether daleel is a fit for your team. It is not sold or shared for advertising purposes, and it is accessible only to members of our team who need it to respond to you. Because our service providers operate in the United States, your information is processed and retained there, where data protection law may differ from that of your jurisdiction.

Automated Enrichment

When you submit the contact form, daleel may run the submitted information through automated data enrichment tools to supplement your record with additional publicly available business information before a member of our team reviews it. This process only adds information to your record; it does not itself determine whether daleel provides any service, set pricing, or produce any decision with a legal or similarly significant effect on you. A human member of our team reviews the enriched record and independently decides whether and how to follow up.

Legal Basis for Processing

This information is processed on the basis of our legitimate interest in responding to an inquiry you initiated. You have the right to object to this processing at any time by emailing privacy@daleel.fyi.

Data Retention

Form submissions are retained for up to 24 months following the last contact with you, after which they are deleted, or sooner if requested. If your inquiry leads to a business relationship with us, your information will instead be retained under the terms of that relationship.

Your Rights

Depending on where you are located, you may hold rights under an applicable data protection law, which may include the right to access, correct, delete, or object to our use of your information. Contact privacy@daleel.fyi to exercise any of these rights or to reach us with a question. You may also lodge a complaint with your local data protection regulator.

Children’s Privacy

Our site and services are intended for business use by adults and are not directed to children. We do not knowingly collect personal data from anyone under the age of 16.

Changes to This Policy

This policy may change periodically, and any updates will appear on this page.
